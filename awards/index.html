Printing Industries of America (PIA) Premier Print Awards

In 1995, Colonial Printing received a Premier Print Award Certificate of Merit from the Printing Industries of America, the world's largest graphic arts association. The Premier Print Awards is the graphic arts industry's largest and most prestigious worldwide printing competition.



Carlson Craft Awards

Each year Carlson Craft, a national leader in wholesale printing, presents selected dealers with awards for outstanding performance and exceptional sales achievement. For the last eight years, Colonial Printing consistently received recognition.
